# Banned constructions — project overlay

Overlays the house list. Entries here add bans; `## Exceptions` strikes words the
house list would otherwise flag.

## Exceptions

- harness — Microsoft's own noun for a Copilot Studio runtime, from "Choose a
  harness" in their documentation. The house ban targets the verb ("harness the
  power of"). Naming the thing by any other word would misname a product feature
  a reader can look up.
